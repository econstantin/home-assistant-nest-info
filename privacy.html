Privacy policy

This policy covers Home Assistant Nest, a personal integration operated by the owner of this repository to connect their Google Nest devices to their own Home Assistant installation.

Data accessed and purpose

After the owner grants access through Google, the integration accesses the authorized home's and devices' identifiers and names, supported thermostat settings, temperature, humidity, operating status, and device change events. The data is used to display device status, receive updates, and carry out the owner's thermostat commands and configured automations.

Authorization and storage

Google handles account sign-in. Home Assistant stores OAuth credentials and authorization tokens in its configuration to maintain the connection; this website does not receive them or the owner's Google password.

Device data may be stored in Home Assistant's state history, logs, and backups according to the owner's configuration and retention settings. Google Cloud Pub/Sub may retain device events according to the cloud project's subscription settings.

Sharing and service providers

The operator does not sell device data, use it for advertising, or intentionally share it with unrelated third parties. Google processes authorization and device events through its services. Home Assistant processes the authorized device data in the owner's installation. Any further sharing through other integrations depends on the owner's configuration.

This public website

These static information pages contain no forms, analytics scripts, advertising, or application cookies. They do not expose thermostat data or authorization credentials. GitHub hosts the pages and may process visitor information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under the GitHub privacy statement.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the integration's access through their Google account connections and remove the Nest integration from Home Assistant. Removing the integration may not delete historical records or backups. The owner can delete those separately using Home Assistant's data management tools, backup controls, and Google Cloud resource settings.

Contact and changes

Questions or deletion requests can be raised through a GitHub issue. Public issues must not contain private device information or credentials. Updates to this policy will be published on this page.
